Cricket Learner AI

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 21, 2026

Information We Collect

Cricket Learner AI may collect email address, name, mobile number, username, player role, lesson progress, quiz answers, assistant chat content, subscription status, and safety moderation records.

How We Use Information

We use this information to create learner accounts, personalize cricket lessons, save progress, provide quizzes and mini games, answer assistant questions, manage Pro Learning access, and protect the app from misuse.

Payments

For direct web versions, payment details may be processed by Razorpay when live payment services are connected. For the Google Play Store version, digital Pro Learning payments should use Google Play Billing. Cricket Learner AI should not store full card details.

AI Assistant

When live AI services are connected, assistant questions and limited learning context may be sent to the AI service endpoint so the assistant can answer. Do not enter sensitive personal information into chat.

Admin Safety

Learner content is not shown to other learners. Verified admin access may review learner details and chat content only for safety, moderation, and support.

Security

Production versions should use HTTPS, server-side password hashing, secure backend authorization, and server-side payment verification.

Contact

For privacy requests, contact the app owner at the support email configured for the production app.
