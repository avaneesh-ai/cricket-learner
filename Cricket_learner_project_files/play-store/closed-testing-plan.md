# Closed Testing Plan

Use this if Play Console requires closed testing before production.

## Tester Group

- Recruit at least 12 testers with Google accounts.
- Ask testers to opt in and stay opted in for 14 continuous days.
- Share the closed test opt-in link from Play Console.
- Keep a simple feedback channel, such as email, WhatsApp, or Google Form.

## What Testers Should Try

- Create account with email, name, mobile number, username, and password.
- Choose all-rounder, bowler, and batsman paths across different testers.
- Play the AI voice intro.
- Ask the assistant cricket and general questions.
- Try daily quiz and weekly quiz.
- Open Videos.
- Play mini games.
- Confirm admin screen is hidden from normal users.
- Install and reopen the app from launcher.
- Test offline reopen after first load.

## Feedback Questions

- Was onboarding easy?
- Did the cricket lessons feel clear?
- Did the assistant answer useful doubts?
- Did voice mode work on your phone?
- Did quizzes and mini games work?
- Did anything overlap or look broken?
- What should improve before public release?

## Production Access Notes

Keep notes on tester feedback and changes you made. Play Console may ask how testers used the app, what feedback you received, and what you changed before applying for production access.
