## App Name

Cricket Learner AI

## Short Description

## Full Description

Cricket Learner AI helps learners understand cricket from basics to advanced match thinking. Choose your player path as an all-rounder, batsman, or bowler and learn with structured lessons, embedded video coaching, daily and weekly quizzes, mini games, past match stories, and a cricket-first AI assistant.

Features:

- Role-based lessons for all-rounders, batsmen, and bowlers
- Batting topics including defence, drives, strokes, lofted shots, and shot selection
- Bowling topics including fast bowling, spin bowling, bowling plans, and pressure overs
- Chat with an AI cricket assistant
- Embedded YouTube coaching videos
- Daily quiz and weekly quiz
- Mini games and pressure decision challenges
- Pro Learning path prepared for compliant billing
- Owner-only admin route for learner safety and moderation
